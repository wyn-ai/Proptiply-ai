How the numbers are counted

Read this before quoting a figure.

Deal

One buy or one sale. A client who sells an HDB and buys a condo counts once per side handled. A referral that closed counts as one deal.

Transacted

OTP exercised, completed, keys handed over, or confirmed sold.

In progress

OTP issued or option fee paid, not yet exercised.

Lost

The client went with another agent, bought or sold on their own, or withdrew the listing.

Deal month

Taken from the OTP date, else the exercise date, else the month typed in. Completion dates are not used as the deal month.

The 8 stages

1 Work in progress (agent assigned, talking to client) · 2 Marketing & staged (listed on PropertyGuru, unit staged) · 3 OTP issued (OTP copy sent to client) · 4 OTP exercised · 5 Completed · 6 Invoiced (invoice number sent to Finance) · 7 Invoice paid (agent has paid) · 8 Lost. Stages 1–3 count as "In progress", 4–7 as "Transacted".

Pinnacle share

What Pinnacle gets from a deal: its cut of the agent's commission, as in the finance sheet. Summed for transacted and in-progress deals, including shares not yet received (e.g. new launches paid next year). Deals without a figure add nothing.

Property value

Sum of property prices on record for transacted and in-progress deals. Deals without a price add nothing, so this is a floor.

Agent

The community agent handling the client. Add new agents on the Agents tab; each gets their own page and link.

Where data comes from
